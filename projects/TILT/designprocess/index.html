THE JOURNEY

01

Observe the problem.

Desk workers often correct posture only after discomfort becomes obvious. The early research asked how a physical object could offer quieter, more timely support.

02

Turn a laptop stand into a teammate.

Plant-inspired form studies established a softer, non-intrusive character—one that can move with the user rather than command them.

03

Engineer a compact response.

Mechanical iterations brought the camera, motors, electronics, and power system into a low-profile base while preserving pitch, roll, and yaw movement.

04

Validate before movement.

MediaPipe provides fast landmark-based estimation; VLM-assisted validation helps confirm sustained posture changes before the stand reacts.
